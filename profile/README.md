# Vaulto

**Your passwords behind a key only you hold.**

Vaulto is a password manager with end-to-end encryption. Your logins live in an encrypted vault that only your master password can open, and that password is not stored anywhere. Available for iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows.

[Website](https://vaulto.com.br/en) · [Download](https://vaulto.com.br/en/download) · [Security](https://vaulto.com.br/en/security) · [Pricing](https://vaulto.com.br/en/pricing) · [For business](https://vaulto.com.br/en/business)

## Highlights

- **Encrypted twice**: on your device before it travels, and again before it is stored ([how it works](https://vaulto.com.br/en/security)).
- **Locks on its own**: after idle time, when you leave the app, or after a number of passwords used.
- **Biometrics and email confirmation**: optional biometrics to copy or reveal, and a code sent by email for new devices.
- **Folders, favorites and search** to keep logins organized.
- **[Password generator](https://vaulto.com.br/en/features/password-generator)**: 4 to 50 characters, with strength shown before saving. Runs in the browser and sends nothing anywhere.

## Encryption

AES-256-GCM · ECDH P-521 · HKDF-SHA256 · PBKDF2-SHA256 (310,000 iterations)
